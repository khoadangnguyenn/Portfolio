abstract: >-
  Real-time navigation in dynamic, mapless environments requires perception models that are both fast and reliable.
  We present MARS, a lightweight multi-task framework that jointly predicts future ego trajectory and dynamic collision risk
  from temporal point-cloud Bird’s-Eye-View features. This setting is relevant to broader robotic sensing pipelines,
  including aerial perception, multimodal robot learning, and BEV-based processing of depth or LiDAR observations.
  MARS couples a PointPillars encoder with a Mamba-based temporal backbone and two lightweight task heads, enabling
  shared spatiotemporal reasoning with low computational cost. Across the compared backbones, MARS achieves the lowest
  inference latency at 3.398 ms while maintaining competitive prediction quality. Among the tested multi-task models,
  it attains the highest <i>AUC</i><sub>1s</sub> for short-horizon risk discrimination and lower global trajectory error
  than the recurrent baselines. These results indicate that MARS is a practical choice for safety-aware navigation on
  resource-constrained robotic platforms.
